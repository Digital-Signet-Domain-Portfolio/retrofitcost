Grant stack: BUS + ECO4 + GBIS + 0% VAT (UK 2026)

How the four live UK retrofit grants stack on the same project in 2026, in the order Ofgem and DESNZ apply them. ECO4 closes 31 December 2026.

Grant stack ladder

  2. ECO4 (closing 31 Dec 2026)Whole-house treatment, £8k to £12k indicativeLow-income, health condition, LA Flex routesClosing soon

  5. Warm Homes Plan + Warm Homes LoanTBC, £5bn envelopeEarly 2027Incoming

Stacking rules

BUS and ECO4 do not double-count toward the same measure but can sit on the same project (heating versus fabric). GBIS is a single-measure scheme and is mutually exclusive with ECO4 for the measure in question. 0% VAT applies at invoice on top of any grant. The Warm Homes Plan and Warm Homes Loan picks up after ECO4 sunsets; sequence works planned for early 2027 around that handover.
