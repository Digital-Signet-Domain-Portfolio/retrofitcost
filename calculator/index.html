Live math, coefficients on /sources/. Gas standing charge comes off the bill first, the rest buys gas at 7.97p, the boiler turns that into heat at the efficiency you set, and the heat pump buys the same heat as electricity at 26.32p divided by the SPF you set. At this cap gas at 85% costs 9.38p per kWh of heat and a heat pump at SPF 2.78 costs 9.47p, which is why the standing charge does most of the work. Payback is quoted on the heat-pump leg alone: the fabric, glazing and solar legs all cut demand too, and this file does not model that, so it publishes no whole-project payback.

What this calculator does

It takes archetype, internal floor area, current and target EPC band, current annual gas spend, and grant eligibility, and returns four numbers: total capex (low to high), grant offset, net cost after grants, and the annual running-cost change from the gas-to-electricity fuel switch at the Q4 2026 Ofgem cap.

It does not pretend to replace a Retrofit Assessor (£400 to £800) or Coordinator (£1,500 to £3,500). It frames the conversation before those fees are spent.

Coefficients

Every coefficient is exposed on the sources manifest as a JSON object with the publishing URL beside it. The math is not proprietary; it is a transparent stack of published unit costs.
