What this calculator does

It takes archetype, internal floor area, current and target EPC band, current annual gas spend, and grant eligibility, and returns four numbers: total capex (low to high), grant offset, net cost after grants, and payback years at the Q3 2026 Ofgem cap.

It does not pretend to replace a Retrofit Assessor (£400 to £800) or Coordinator (£1,500 to £3,500). It frames the conversation before those fees are spent.

Preset routes

  • Deep retrofit versus heat pump only
  • Grant stack: BUS, ECO4, GBIS, 0% VAT
  • Payback at Q3 2026 Ofgem cap

Coefficients

Every coefficient is exposed on the sources manifest as a JSON object with the publishing URL beside it. The math is not proprietary; it is a transparent stack of published unit costs.
