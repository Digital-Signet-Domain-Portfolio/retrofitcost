Air-source heat pump cost (2026)

Air-source heat pump cost: installed cost range with each end sourced, BUS grant treatment, payback shape.

Cost in 2026

Air-source heat pump cost (2026). The published installed range in 2026 is £10,500 to £18,000 installed for a 7 to 12 kW unit (DESNZ guidance and MCS Installation Database). Net after the £7,500 BUS grant: £3,000 to £10,500. Both ends of that range are stated rather than collapsed to a midpoint, because the spread reflects real differences in scope, access and grant eligibility rather than installer mark-up.

What drives the spread

  • Unit kW size; oversize is the most common cost error.
  • Refrigerant: R32 vs R290 propane (the R290 units are now in volume).
  • Cylinder swap to indirect plus immersion.
  • Pipework upgrade if microbore.
  • Radiator upsize for low-temp design (often a separate £1,800 to £4,500 line).

Where it sits in the PAS 2035 sequence

Heating stratum, third in PAS 2035 sequence.
