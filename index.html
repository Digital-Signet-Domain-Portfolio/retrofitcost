Direct answer

Three UK suppliers publish a post-grant price for an air-source heat pump: Octopus £4,064 as its typical figure, E.ON Next from £4,946, Aira £6,729 as a worked example, all after the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant. A phased deep retrofit across fabric, ventilation, heating, renewables and controls under PAS 2035 runs £35,000 to £70,000. What the heat pump will not do is pay for itself out of running costs: at the cap for 1 October to 31 December 2026, a gas boiler at 85% delivers heat at 9.38p per kWh and a heat pump at the national trial median SPF of 2.78 delivers it at 9.47p. The grant is the case, not the fuel bill.

Risk path before measure list.

PAS 2035 tiers projects by EPC band and traditional construction. The path determines whether a Retrofit Designer is mandatory and how much survey work the Coordinator carries. The path is set on day one and cannot be unset.

Path A, EPC D and above, post-1919, no protected featuresPath B, Pre-1919 or below EPC D, or two or more measuresPath C, Traditional construction, listed, system-built, or three or more measures
