Post-2000 new-build retrofit cost (UK 2026)

Post-2000 new-build retrofit cost: working capex envelope, sequenced measure cost, PAS 2035 risk path.

Working envelope (100 m² post-2000 build)

Scope is narrow. Typical capex: £11,000to£25,000(pre-grant). Net after BUS: £3,500to£17,500. The case for fabric work is usually limited; PV plus battery plus HP is the modal stack.

Sequenced cost

  • ASHP plus rads £8,000 to £14,000 net of BUS.
  • Solar PV 4 kWp £5,800 to £8,800.
  • Battery 5 kWh £3,500 to £5,250.
  • MVHR retrofit possible in well-built post-2010 stock.

Risk path

Path A, post-2000 build
