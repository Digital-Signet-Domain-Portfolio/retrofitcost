Retrofit Assessor cost (UK 2026)

Retrofit Assessor cost (UK 2026): working anatomy of the fee line, deliverables, when mandatory.

Cost in 2026

A PAS 2035 Retrofit Assessor charges £400to£800 for a single property. The deliverable is an RdSAP energy assessment, an occupancy assessment and a condition report.

What you get back

  • RdSAP file with EPC band before and modelled after.
  • Occupancy assessment (vulnerable persons, heating habits).
  • Condition report flagging damp, structure, services.
  • Improvement Option Evaluation feeding into the MTIP.

Where to find one

The TrustMark directory and Retrofit Academy directory list PAS 2035 Assessors by region. Many Assessors trade as one-person businesses; lead times in 2026 are typically two to six weeks.
